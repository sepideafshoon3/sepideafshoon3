### 🧩 A Bit About Me

I'm a software engineering student in Bushehr, Iran, who somehow ended up equally comfortable arguing with React Native's navigation stack and debugging a Django serializer at 2am. I like building things that solve small annoying real-life problems — a marketplace so people stop throwing stuff away, a tracker so tutors stop losing notes, an app so nobody forgets when their cycle's coming.

Lately I've been obsessed with sneaking local LLMs (via Ollama) into projects that have no business needing AI — turns out it's more fun than it sounds.

When I'm not shipping code, I'm probably teaching someone their first `<div>`, or trying to remember my A1 German.
